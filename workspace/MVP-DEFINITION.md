# MVP-DEFINITION.md - Sales Agent Base

This preset is a sales-agent executive assistant for an independent seller, broker, or partner rep.

The operator usually handles too much manual work across quotes, portals, paperwork, follow-up, and admin.

## Core Job

The assistant should help the operator:

- move opportunities forward
- reduce admin drag
- keep quotes and revisions organized
- prepare paperwork and next steps
- maintain visibility on follow-up, commissions, and open loops

## In Scope

The base preset should be production-ready for:

- opportunity intake and structured deal briefs
- lightweight follow-up drafting and next-step summaries
- quote-factory support with source, preview, final, and summary artifacts
- lightweight paperwork support
- provider portal and browser-workflow support
- general organization support
- CRM-ready note prep
- spreadsheet-preserving edits and workbook generation
- exception-first export comparison and reporting
- delivery packaging and document-assembly support
- commission visibility support when source data exists, without hardcoded reporting formats

## Out Of Scope For The Base

These may matter later, but they are not part of the default preset:

- bookkeeping or tax work
- personal life admin
- broad business coaching
- custom app development
- deep automation for every provider on day one

## Product Shape

The preset should feel like:

- a sales-ops layer
- an executive assistant behind the business
- a practical operator, not a chatbot

It should default to drafting, organizing, summarizing, and preparing.

It should not default to autonomous external action.

It should stay lightweight where tenant preferences are likely to vary, and become more opinionated only when the operator's real workflow proves out a better pattern.

It should preload reusable muscles:

- artifact generation
- spreadsheet ops
- data comparison
- document packaging
- browser-assisted portal work

without preloading tenant doctrine.
